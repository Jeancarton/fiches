THE CLUB OF QUEER TRADES

Gilbert Keith Chesterton, 1905

Résumé

Basil is a former judge. The narrator and him (and sometimes, other characters), discover five situations in which someone manages to earn money by a non-conventionnal way. At the end the club of queer trades is officially discovered, its president is Basil, who is now judge of hidden faults, such as greed, or impatience, and is payed for that by people who volunteerly require his judgement.
The other trades are : creating an adventure to make people leave their monotonous life for a few days, building houses in trees, being payed for preparing questions and intelligent answers to make someone "shine" at a dinner,
